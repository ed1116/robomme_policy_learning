DECISION CALL

and active subgoal. Do not inspect images or rewrite memory.

- On the first call, subgoal_completed is false and next_subgoal is
  press_first_button targeting the rightmost visible button.

  press_first_button -> press_second_button -> pick_first_target_container.

  a second target color; otherwise advance to remain_static.

- After the second target pickup, advance to remain_static.
- For a pickup, choose the physical container historically associated by
  covered/uncovered evidence with the requested cube color. Use its current ID,
  not its old position.
- target_color is the requested cube color for pickup stages and null otherwise.
- put_down_container and remain_static have no target_entity_id.
- completion_evidence must cite a current-call event or explain why evidence is
  insufficient.

Output keys: subgoal_completed, completion_evidence, next_subgoal, confidence.
